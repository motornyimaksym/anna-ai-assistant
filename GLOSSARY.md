# Glossary

- **Publicly available service** — an enabled catalog service the owner has explicitly opted into public booking with `publiclyAvailable`. It is offered on `/book` and to new Telegram clients; the FAQ catalog and returning-client rules stay unchanged.
- **Payment hold** — a temporary claim on a selected studio time while the client pays the UAH 500 deposit. It lasts 15 minutes and does not confirm an appointment.
- **Deposit** — the UAH 500 paid through Monobank for every public booking. It is credited toward the selected service's full price.
